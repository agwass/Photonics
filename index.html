Our story

Meet Jerry Baraza

Jerry Baraza
Electrical Engineer – Fibre Optics Technology

Jerry Baraza is a seasoned Electrical Engineer with a deep specialisation in Fibre Optics Technology. With over a decade of hands‑on experience, he has worked on fibre optic infrastructure projects across East Africa, Southern Africa, and the Middle East.

He serves as a technical consultant for several African governments, advising on national broadband strategies, fibre backbone deployments, and policy frameworks that are shaping the continent's digital future. His work has taken him from rural connectivity projects to urban metropolitan fibre rings, always with a focus on sustainable, high‑quality infrastructure.

  • Consultant to African governments
